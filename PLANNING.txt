1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Build a complete, integrated, web-based CRM for ReeferON to manage the full
customer acquisition and customer management process.

Three major modules (MUST stay connected — not separate systems):

  1. Sales Prospect Management
  2. New Customer Onboarding
  3. Existing Customer Master

Main business workflow:

  New Lead
    → Sales Process
    → Commercial Approval
    → Customer Onboarding
    → Department Approvals
    → Customer Code Generation
    → Existing Customer Master
    → Customer Activation

IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT RULE
- Do NOT build these as independent forms or separate systems.
- Prospect data must auto-transfer to Onboarding, then to Customer Master
  after required approvals.
- Never force users to re-enter the same information.

7. DEPARTMENT APPROVAL MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Finance approval

Actions: review docs, approve/reject items, remarks, request corrections, resubmit

7.2 Operations approval
Verify: Service availability, vehicle, storage, origin/destination,
        route feasibility, operational readiness
Approve / reject with remarks

7.3 Commercial approval
Verify: Approved rates, commercial proposal, customer agreement,
        commercial reference, other commercial conditions

7.4 Final Admin approval
After all department approvals, Admin reviews full package and
approves or rejects with reason.

Approval statuses: Pending / Approved / Rejected

Rules:
- Capture approver name, date, time, remarks
- Rejected → correction → resubmit
- Full approval history
- Customer code DISABLED until all mandatory + final approval done
- UI must show which department is pending

8. AUTOMATIC CUSTOMER CODE GENERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Generate unique customer code ONLY after final onboarding approval.

Format:
  REO-CUS-0001
  REO-CUS-0002
  REO-CUS-0003

Process:
  1. Sales submits onboarding
  2. Finance verifies
  3. Operations confirms readiness
  4. Commercial verifies commercials
  5. Admin final approval
  6. CRM auto-generates customer code
  7. Record transfers to Existing Customer Master
  8. Authorised user can activate customer

Rules:
- Codes unique; users cannot edit them
- No duplicate generation
- Database-controlled sequence
- Safe under simultaneous approvals
- On failure: no incomplete / duplicate customer record

19. HOW THIS CRM WORKS (BEGINNER GUIDE + EXAMPLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What is a CRM?
CRM = Customer Relationship Management.
Simple meaning: one software where Sales keeps every lead, every call,
every approval, and every customer — so data is not lost in Excel,
WhatsApp or notebooks.

Think of it like a school admission file:
  Lead (interested student) → interviews → documents → principal approval
  → admission number → student register.
Same idea for ReeferON customers.

Three big boxes (connected, not separate):

  BOX 1 – Prospect (Sales lead)
    "Someone may give us business."

  BOX 2 – Onboarding (paperwork + approvals)
    "Deal is almost done; collect docs and get approvals."

  BOX 3 – Customer Master (official customer)
    "Approved customer with unique code REO-CUS-xxxx."

EXAMPLE STORY (Ice cream company)

Day 1 – New Lead
  Salesperson Rahul meets "CoolTreat Ice Cream Pvt Ltd" in Pune.
  They need Frozen transportation (14ft vehicles).
  Rahul opens CRM → Create Prospect.
  System gives Prospect ID: REO-LEAD-0042
  Company, contact, mobile, service, temperature saved.
  WHY useful: lead is now in system, not only in Rahul's phone.

Day 2–10 – Sales Process (Pipeline)
  Rahul updates stage:
    New Prospect → Calling → Meeting → Proposal Sent
    → Negotiation → Commercial Sent
  He also sets Next Follow-up Date (e.g. call again Friday).
  WHY useful: Manager sees "Rahul has 5 overdue follow-ups"
  and "CoolTreat is in Negotiation" without asking WhatsApp.

Day 11 – Commercial Approved
  Customer says yes to rates.
  Rahul sets stage = Commercial Approved.
  Button appears: Initiate Customer Onboarding.
  WHY useful: only serious deals enter paperwork; random leads don't.

Day 12 – Onboarding (no retyping)
  CRM copies company name, contact, mobile, service, temperature
  from the prospect automatically.
  Rahul only adds GSTIN, addresses, vehicle size, origin/destination,
  uploads GST/PAN/KYC/Agreement/PO.
  WHY useful: same company details typed only once → fewer mistakes.

Day 13–15 – Approvals
  Finance checks GST/KYC/payment terms/credit limit.
  Operations checks vehicle/route/storage readiness.
  Commercial checks rates/agreement.
  Admin gives final approval.
  WHY useful: controlled process; wrong or incomplete customer
  cannot get a customer code.

Day 16 – Customer Code + Customer Master
  System creates REO-CUS-0187 automatically.
  CoolTreat appears in Existing Customer Master as Active.
  Prospect stage becomes Customer Activated.
  WHY useful: one official customer list for whole company;
  Ops/Finance can search REO-CUS-0187 later.

Also supported:
  - Existing old customers can be added directly to Customer Master
    (skip prospect pipeline), with duplicate check on GSTIN + name.
  - Ordinary users cannot permanently delete; use Inactive / Cancelled.
